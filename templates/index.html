CULINARY EXCELLENCE

DINING AT HOTEL COASTAL PALACE

Two distinctive culinary traditions curated under one roof. Savor the authentic ocean bounty at Coastal Flavours or enjoy pristine South Indian vegetarian dishes at Krishna Swaad.

PURE VEGETARIAN

Krishna Swaad Pure Veg

🕒 6:30 AM – 10:30 PM · Pure Ghee Preparations

Serving traditional South Indian breakfast delicacies including paper-crisp Masala Dosas, golden Medu Vadas, fluffy Poori Bhajis, Pineapple Kesari Bath, and piping hot Chikmagalur Filter Coffee.

SEAFOOD SPECIALIST

Coastal Flavours Sea Food

🕒 11:30 AM – 11:00 PM · Fresh Malpe Catch

Savor fresh coastal seafood sourced daily from the docks of Malpe. Experience signature tawa-fried anjal, spiced squid kalamari roast, Mangalorean kori sukka, neer dosa, and traditional banana leaf feasts.

WARM AMBIENCE

The Complete Family Dining Experience

Whether joining us for an energizing breakfast with filter coffee or an evening seafood celebration, our expansive AC dining halls with hand-crafted jali screens provide comfortable, spacious seating for families.

ABOUT US

Warm Coastal Hospitality

Located at Pandubettu, Kalmadi, Mudanidambur, Karnataka, Hotel Coastal Palace is designed to offer discerning travelers, families, and business guests effortless comfort and authentic hospitality.

With close proximity to Malpe Beach, the revered Kalmadi Church, and key transit hubs, our property provides contemporary double and deluxe accommodations paired with two celebrated dining destinations right downstairs.
